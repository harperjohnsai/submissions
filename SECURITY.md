# Private and sensitive reports

Most submissions belong in a public issue. Use a private channel when publishing would cause harm, for example:

- a lead on an **active campaign** where a public post could tip off the operator;
- a **legitimate domain, file or account wrongly listed** as malicious in our published IOCs or rules;
- a **security problem in our tools** (extractors, scripts) in
  [harperjohnsai/detections](https://github.com/harperjohnsai/detections);
- a complaint or abuse inquiry about content we published.

## Channels

1. **GitHub private report** (preferred if you have a GitHub account): the
   [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/harperjohnsai/submissions/security/advisories/new) button on the
   Security tab. Only you and the maintainers can see it.
2. **Email**: harperjohnspi@gmail.com with the subject `[private] <short description>`.

Same rules as everywhere else: **hashes and defanged links, never files**; no personal or stolen data. If a sample is
involved, upload it to [MalwareBazaar](https://bazaar.abuse.ch/) or [tria.ge](https://tria.ge/) first and send the
SHA-256. If you do not want the sample to be public at all, tell us the hash and context and we will tell you what we
can do without the file.

Private reports are read by a human before anything is published. We do not share your identity with anyone.
Expect a reply within a few days (best effort).
